◆ Side project · Live demo2026

EventPass, QR check-in with AI follow-ups

Guests check in to live events by QR code and a one-time email code. Each guest who opts in gets one AI-written follow-up afterwards.

NextJSTypeScriptSupabaseAIUK GDPR
Features and demo
Role
Fullstack, solo
Demo
eventpass-demo-phi.vercel.app ↗
  • Organizers create an event and get a QR code, a printable poster and a full-screen present mode for the projector.
  • Guests join with a QR code and an emailed one-time code. Follow-ups stay off until the guest opts in.
  • AI writes each follow-up from the guest's profile, goals and organizer notes. Every message has an unsubscribe link, and failed sends retry up to three times.
  • Privacy is enforced in the database: organizers see name, company, job title and follow-up status, while contact details stay private. Consent is recorded with the policy version, and guests can delete their data at any time. Data is also deleted automatically after the event.
